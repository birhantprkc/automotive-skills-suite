### Why #46 was descoped rather than carried again

The original DoD — audit *every* builder-to-builder and builder-to-reviewer handoff across 76
pairs — is a multi-day job that has never fit a single polish day, which is exactly why it has
never been started. The descoped version covers the failure mode that actually bit us: #43 was a
builder-to-builder read whose expected sheet names had drifted from the emitted ones, and it sat
broken for four weeks because nothing was checking. Builder-to-reviewer pairs are a different and
lower-risk shape (the reviewer is written against the builder it ships with) and are deferred.

If Tuesday still cannot finish the descoped version, the honest conclusion is that #46 needs a
human decision about scope, not a sixth carry — and that should be written into the polish log
rather than rolled forward silently.
