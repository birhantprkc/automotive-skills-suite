## Was September a good month?

**Mixed. The engineering quality was the best yet, but there was less of it and it was less reliable.**

The good: the work moved from fixing symptoms to building detectors. The #64 column-level chain audit and the repo-wide pyflakes sweep turned "chain breaks zero" from August into a measured backlog of five concrete, reproducible defects (#65–#69 class). Two skills that had never worked were found. The `mbse` and `v&v` starvation from August was cleared exactly as recommended. Polish logs pushed back on their own issues when a framing was wrong: #63 was downgraded HIGH → MED after the "live symptoms" turned out not to be read by any consumer.

The bad: commits fell to 25, eight days were lost to missed runs, two weekly releases were skipped, and the issue board was only kept honest by a human batch close. Three of four August recommendations were not done: the heartbeat, the close-on-commit step and the staleness definition. The only one that was done (pin `mbse`/`v&v`) was the only one an autonomous PLAN run could do by itself.
